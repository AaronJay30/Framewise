# FrameWise Logo Concepts

## Longlist

1. **Open Crop** — four quiet framing corners hold a coral focal point.
2. **Golden Point** — a nested composition path ends at one decisive coral
   point.
3. **Framing Fold** — an abstract F/W fold turns the name into a compact
   symbol.
4. **Sightline** — a single diagonal route crosses a stable frame.
5. **Horizon Pin** — a calm horizon bar and offset point suggest landscape
   composition.
6. **Split Monogram** — two interlocking planes form an F and W without a
   literal camera.
7. **Aperture Notch** — a square frame becomes an identifying notch around a
   focal point.
8. **Quiet Corner** — one coral corner interrupts an ink field as a restrained
   alignment cue.

## Selected concepts

| Concept | Type | Clarity | Distinction | Simplicity | Relevance | Small-size | Total |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Open Crop | Abstract / combination | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 24 |
| Framing Fold | Letterform / abstract | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 21 |
| Sightline | Abstract / pictorial | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 21 |

**Recommendation:** Open Crop. It is the clearest expression of FrameWise's
core promise, works without the wordmark, and gives the coral accent a useful
role as the point the user is trying to place.
