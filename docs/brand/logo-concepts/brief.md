# FrameWise Logo Exploration Brief

## Working brief

- **Name:** FrameWise
- **Product:** An offline-first iPhone camera that helps people compose
  better photographs in real time.
- **Audience:** People who want calm, practical guidance while taking photos,
  from casual photographers to visually minded creators.
- **Brand qualities:** Calm, precise, modern, focused, trustworthy.
- **Primary uses:** iOS app opener, app icon exploration, camera UI, and
  compact digital surfaces.
- **Required palette:** Ink `#10151B` and Signal Coral `#FF6B57` only.
- **Form constraints:** Flat geometry, no gradients, no shadows, no
  decorative AI effects, and a symbol that remains recognizable at small
  sizes.
- **Strategic direction:** Evoke framing, alignment, and visual judgment
  without drawing a literal camera or lens.

## Assumptions

The fast-track exploration assumes a symbol-first combination mark will be
more useful than a long wordmark because the app needs an icon and a quiet
launch opener. The wordmark shown in these explorations uses live system text
for comparison only; any selected direction must be outlined or rebuilt as
paths before final delivery.
